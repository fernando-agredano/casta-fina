## Design Context

### Users
Streetwear cap buyers, 18-35, urban/Mexican audience (site copy is in Spanish, brand based in Guadalajara). They browse casually on phone and desktop, value exclusivity/limited drops and authenticity over mass-market volume.

### Brand Personality
"Casta Fina" = pedigree/lineage-inspired streetwear. Voice: disciplined, street, ambitious ("No todos cargan presencia. Algunos la construyen."). Certificate/provenance motif — serial numbers, "libro de origen", authenticity cards — applied to an otherwise dark urban streetwear look.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark theme only (near-black, cool-tinted background, not warm). Accent color is **electric blue ("cobalt")**, used sparingly for links/labels/highlights; a secondary **oxblood** red exists for rare CTA emphasis (e.g. the "Unirme" button). Typography: **Big Shoulders** (condensed display, headlines/logo, uppercase) paired with **Archivo** (body, semibold/bold — no italics, no script/serif). Corners are medium-rounded (not sharp, not pill-everywhere) — `rounded-lg`/`rounded-xl`/`rounded-2xl` per element size. Product photography is real, dark studio shots of black caps plus one b/w street lifestyle photo used in the hero.

### Design Principles
1. Bold condensed display type for headlines, plain-weight (non-italic) Archivo for everything else — thickness over cursive.
2. Cobalt is the only "loud" accent color; keep neutrals cool-dark, not warm.
3. Section rhythm: outer `<section>` gets `px-6 md:px-16` + vertical padding, inner content wrapped in `mx-auto max-w-7xl` (no double-padding) — this must stay pixel-identical across Navbar and every section.
4. Corners: medium radius everywhere (buttons, cards, image frames), never fully sharp, never full-pill except small badges/dots.
5. Real product photography crops matter — most source photos are landscape (~16:9); avoid tall portrait crops that cut off the cap.
